# Amisulpride — D2/D3 occupancy and the low-dose presynaptic hypothesis

**Status:** VERIFIED MECHANISTIC HUMAN EVIDENCE  
**Evidence grade:** C for the simplified “low-dose pro-dopaminergic” model  
**Role:** comparator to L-DOPA/lurasidone, not direct extinction evidence

## Key sources

### Xiberas et al. 2001
*In vivo extrastriatal and striatal D2 dopamine receptor blockade by amisulpride in schizophrenia.*

- PMID: 11270918
- DOI: 10.1097/00004714-200104000-00013
- Doses in study: 25–1,200 mg/day.
- Lower plasma concentrations (28–92 ng/mL) were associated with marked extrastriatal and relatively low striatal binding; higher concentrations produced marked binding in both.

### Martinot et al. 1996
*In vivo characteristics of dopamine D2 receptor occupancy by amisulpride in schizophrenia.*

- PMID: 8935811
- DOI: 10.1007/BF02245616
- PET study in 11 patients examining dose vs striatal D2 occupancy.

### Meisenzahl et al. / low- vs high-dose SPECT
*D2 receptor occupancy during high- and low-dose therapy with the atypical antipsychotic amisulpride.*

- PMID: 15937316
- Low-dose group: 50–300 mg/day, mean ~229 mg/day.
- Even the low-dose group showed substantial postsynaptic striatal D2 occupancy, though lower than high-dose treatment.

### Vernaleken et al. 2004/2005
*High striatal occupancy of D2-like dopamine receptors by amisulpride in the brain of patients with schizophrenia.*

- PMID: 15683553
- DOI: 10.1017/S1461145704004353
- Occupancy correlated strongly with plasma concentration; high striatal occupancy could occur across clinically used exposure ranges.

## FearPrime interpretation
The traditional model is:
- low exposure → preferential presynaptic D2/D3 autoreceptor blockade → increased dopamine release;
- high exposure → stronger postsynaptic D2/D3 blockade → antipsychotic effect.

The imaging literature supports **dose/plasma-dependent regional differences**, but it does not justify treating 25–50 mg amisulpride as a pure presynaptic or guaranteed pro-dopaminergic intervention.

Postsynaptic occupancy, extrastriatal occupancy, individual pharmacokinetics and prolactin effects make the real pharmacology more complicated.

## Direct fear-extinction evidence
At present in the FearPrime ledger:
**no verified direct human amisulpride fear-extinction study has been established.**

## FearPrime weight
Use amisulpride as a **mechanistic comparator/hypothesis**, not as evidence-equivalent to L-DOPA.
