# Epigenetics in fear extinction and PTSD

**Evidence grade:** B-D depending on subtrack  
**Track:** methylation / histone acetylation / stress-plasticity genes

## COMT genotype + methylation in PTSD fear inhibition

**Title:** Differential Genetic and Epigenetic Regulation of catechol-O-methyltransferase is Associated with Impaired Fear Inhibition in Posttraumatic Stress Disorder

- PMID: 23596403
- Human PTSD/trauma sample.
- Fear-potentiated startle conditioning/extinction.
- COMT Val158Met genotype plus promoter methylation.

### Main result
Met/Met genotype was associated with greater safety-signal fear and poorer extinction-related inhibition. Specific COMT promoter CpG methylation patterns were also associated with impaired fear inhibition.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23596403/

## GR–FKBP51 complex in PTSD

**Title:** The glucocorticoid receptor-FKBP51 complex contributes to fear conditioning and posttraumatic stress disorder

- PMID: 31929189

### Main result
The GR-FKBP51 complex was elevated in PTSD patients and fear-conditioned mice, with associated alterations in glucocorticoid-receptor signaling.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31929189/

## FearPrime interpretation

Epigenetics is not a single intervention category.

At minimum separate:
- histone acetylation,
- histone methylation,
- DNA methylation,
- stress-regulatory gene expression,
- catecholamine-regulatory gene expression.

The strongest direct human causal evidence in this repository remains **acute HDAC-related modulation with sodium butyrate**, whereas COMT/FKBP5 methylation findings are mainly moderator/association evidence.

## FearPrime weight
**Core mechanism layer, but intervention evidence varies greatly by target.**
