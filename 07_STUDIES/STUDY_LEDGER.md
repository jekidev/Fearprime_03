# FearPrime study ledger

This is the canonical evidence ledger. References should not be promoted to VERIFIED until DOI/PMID/title/authors and study design have been checked against the primary source.

## High-priority studies to verify/import

### Sodium butyrate — human extinction study (2026)

**Project memory:** triple-blind randomized study, approximately n=180, 4 g sodium butyrate. Previously interpreted as showing improved 7-day extinction-memory retrieval when extinction learning was strong, without preventing stress reinstatement.

**Status:** NEEDS PRIMARY-SOURCE METADATA VERIFICATION.

**FearPrime implication:** plasticity/consolidation augmentation appears conditional on successful learning; return-of-fear mechanisms should be measured separately.

---

### L-DOPA after extinction

**Project memory:** human experimental literature has been discussed in which L-DOPA administered around/post extinction can improve later extinction retrieval under some conditions. Outcome may depend on quality/success of extinction; unsuccessful extinction raises the possibility of strengthening undesirable learning.

**Status:** NEEDS PRIMARY-SOURCE METADATA VERIFICATION AND STUDY-BY-STUDY EXTRACTION.

**FearPrime implication:** dopamine should be modeled as a learning/consolidation modifier, not a generic anti-fear drug.

---

### Oxytocin + exposure/extinction

**Research target:** identify controlled human studies examining intranasal oxytocin during fear conditioning/extinction or exposure-based treatment. Extract sex/context effects, timing, acquisition, extinction, retrieval and return-of-fear outcomes separately.

**Status:** LITERATURE IMPORT REQUIRED.

---

### D-cycloserine + exposure therapy

**Research target:** use as a benchmark example of why pharmacological enhancement of exposure can depend on session quality and timing.

**Status:** LITERATURE IMPORT REQUIRED.

---

### Lurasidone / 5-HT7 / fear extinction

**Research target:** separate direct human evidence from animal extinction evidence and receptor-level mechanistic inference. Search lurasidone, 5-HT7 antagonism, 5-HT1A partial agonism, BDNF/TrkB-related outcomes, fear conditioning and PTSD.

**Status:** HYPOTHESIS — DIRECT EXTINCTION EVIDENCE NOT YET ESTABLISHED IN THIS LEDGER.

---

### Low-dose amisulpride

**Research target:** verify human PET/receptor occupancy and pharmacodynamic evidence for the presynaptic autoreceptor/pro-dopaminergic low-dose model; distinguish this from prolactin and postsynaptic D2/D3 effects.

**Status:** MECHANISTIC COMPARATOR.

## Extraction template

For every paper record:

- Citation
- DOI
- PMID
- Species
- N
- Design
- Population
- Intervention
- Study dose (literature metadata)
- Timing relative to acquisition/extinction/retrieval
- Comparator
- Primary endpoint
- Immediate extinction result
- Delayed retrieval result
- Renewal result
- Reinstatement result
- Adverse events
- Authors' conclusion
- FearPrime interpretation
- Alternative explanation / debug
- Evidence grade
