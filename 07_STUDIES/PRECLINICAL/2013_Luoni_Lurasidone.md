# Luoni et al. 2013 — Chronic lurasidone in a SERT-knockout fear-extinction model

**Status:** VERIFIED PRECLINICAL PRIMARY STUDY  
**Evidence grade:** C  
**Species:** rat  
**Important:** this is not direct human PTSD evidence.

## Citation
Luoni A, Hulsken S, Cazzaniga G, Racagni G, Homberg JR, Riva MA. *Behavioural and neuroplastic properties of chronic lurasidone treatment in serotonin transporter knockout rats.* International Journal of Neuropsychopharmacology. 2013;16(6):1319-1330.

- PMID: 23164505
- DOI: 10.1017/S1461145712001332
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23164505/

## Design
- 64 female rats across SERT+/+ and SERT-/- groups.
- Vehicle or lurasidone.
- Lurasidone: 10 mg/kg/day orally for 21 days.
- Fear conditioning measured after two weeks of treatment; extinction assessed subsequently.

## Main result
Chronic lurasidone:
- significantly increased fear extinction in SERT-knockout rats;
- did not improve extinction in wild-type rats;
- normalized reduced prefrontal BDNF expression in the SERT-knockout model;
- restored several altered GABAergic markers.

## FearPrime interpretation
This gives **real preclinical support** to the lurasidone track and makes it stronger than a receptor-profile-only hypothesis.

However, the effect was genotype/model-specific and followed chronic treatment. It cannot be translated directly to a human acute-exposure protocol.

Possible mechanistic paths include:
- PFC BDNF normalization,
- GABAergic network normalization,
- serotonergic/dopaminergic receptor interactions,
- 5-HT7 antagonism as one component rather than a proven sole mechanism.

## Debug
- Animal model, not PTSD.
- Female rats.
- 10 mg/kg/day in rats is not a human-equivalent dosing recommendation.
- Chronic 21-day exposure means this study does not test whether a single dose around exposure improves consolidation.
- Wild-type animals did not show the same benefit.

## FearPrime weight
**Meaningful preclinical evidence, low direct clinical certainty.**

## Source audit — 2026-09-15
The discussion also reports increased conditioned freezing during acquisition irrespective of genotype, and no significant genotype difference in extinction deficit in this experiment. Avoid describing the finding as a demonstrated rescue of a significant baseline extinction deficit. See the [updated dossier](../../04_CANDIDATES/LURASIDONE_DOSSIER.md).
