# GLP-1 receptor agonism and PTSD/fear extinction

**Status:** VERIFIED NEW PRECLINICAL PTSD EVIDENCE; DIRECT HUMAN EXTINCTION EVIDENCE NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Evidence grade:** C/D  
**Track:** GLP-1 / metabolic-neuroplastic signaling

## Akillioglu et al. 2026

**Title:** GLP-1 receptor agonism reduces PTSD-like anxiety and alters amygdala-hippocampal activity patterns in a Chemogenetic mouse model

- PMID: 42342196
- DOI: 10.1016/j.pnpbp.2026.111804
- Published 2026.
- Mouse single-prolonged-stress PTSD-like model.
- Liraglutide investigated alongside chemogenetic manipulation of basolateral-amygdala glutamatergic neurons.

## Main result
Liraglutide was associated with:
- improved anxiety-like behavior in the PTSD-like model,
- alterations in hippocampal NR2B,
- CREB-related signaling,
- GLP-1 receptor expression.

Some neural c-Fos findings did not survive false-discovery-rate correction and were appropriately characterized as exploratory.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42342196/

## What this study does NOT establish
The study did not demonstrate:
- human PTSD efficacy,
- human extinction augmentation,
- improved extinction retention,
- reduced renewal/reinstatement,
- or a direct cell-specific GLP-1 mechanism for extinction.

## Human observational context
Recent large observational semaglutide work has examined broad neuropsychiatric outcomes rather than fear-extinction mechanisms. Such associations cannot establish causality or PTSD-specific extinction enhancement.

## FearPrime interpretation

Potential pathways worth tracking:
- GLP-1R → glutamate/NMDA modulation,
- CREB/plasticity signaling,
- metabolic stabilization,
- inflammation modulation,
- hippocampal/amygdala state.

But the current evidence rank should remain below direct human extinction candidates.
