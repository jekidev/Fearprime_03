# Ribbens et al. 2026 — Sodium butyrate and long-term fear extinction

**Status:** VERIFIED PRIMARY HUMAN STUDY  
**Evidence grade:** B — randomized human experimental evidence  
**Population:** healthy participants, not PTSD/CPTSD patients

## Citation
Ribbens C, Peeters L, Van Oudenhove L, Vervliet B, Dalile B. *The HDAC inhibitor sodium butyrate moderately enhances long-term fear extinction memory retrieval in humans.* Molecular Psychiatry. 2026.

- PMID: 42557391
- DOI: 10.1038/s41380-026-03802-1
- Primary: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42557391/
- Journal: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-026-03802-1

## Design
- N = 180 healthy participants.
- Randomized, triple-blind, placebo-controlled, six-arm design.
- 3-day Pavlovian fear-conditioning paradigm with delayed return-of-fear testing.
- 3 × 2 factorial design: pre-extinction NaBu, post-extinction NaBu, or placebo × short vs long extinction.

## Intervention as studied
- Single oral **4 g sodium butyrate (NaBu)** dose.
- Pre-extinction administration: 30 min before extinction.
- Post-extinction administration: immediately after extinction.
- Short extinction: 4 non-reinforced trials per conditioned stimulus.
- Long extinction: 16 non-reinforced trials per conditioned stimulus.

The dose and timing above are study metadata, not a personal treatment recommendation.

## Outcomes
- Long-term extinction-memory retrieval tested seven days later.
- NaBu reduced US-expectancy to CS+ during delayed retrieval relative to placebo.
- No clear treatment effect was detected on SCR for the main retrieval finding.
- NaBu did **not** prevent return of fear in the reinstatement test.
- Benefit emerged when extinction learning was robust/high-trial; NaBu did not rescue the short/subthreshold learning condition.
- Pre-extinction NaBu did not measurably improve within-session extinction acquisition.

## FearPrime interpretation
This is currently the strongest direct human anchor for the FearPrime HDAC/butyrate track.

The result supports:
`successful extinction learning → consolidation window → HDAC-related augmentation → stronger later retrieval`

It does **not** support:
`NaBu → global suppression or erasure of fear`

The effect was bounded: delayed extinction-memory retrieval improved, whereas stress-like reinstatement was not prevented.

## Debug / limitations
- Healthy sample rather than PTSD/CPTSD.
- First direct human study of this specific acute NaBu/extinction design; replication is needed.
- Behavioral US-expectancy and physiological SCR did not align across all analyses.
- The short-extinction manipulation did not fully produce the intended impaired-retention phenotype.
- Mechanistic HDAC interpretation is biologically plausible but the human experiment did not directly prove brain histone-acetylation mediation.

## FearPrime weight
**High priority.** Use as a core study, while retaining the boundary condition: plasticity enhancement was useful only after sufficiently robust learning.
