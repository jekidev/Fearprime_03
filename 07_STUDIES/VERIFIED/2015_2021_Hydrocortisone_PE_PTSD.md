# Hydrocortisone augmentation of Prolonged Exposure in PTSD — pilot vs larger RCT

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN CLINICAL EVIDENCE  
**Evidence grade:** A/B  
**Track:** glucocorticoids / exposure augmentation

## Study 1 — Yehuda et al. 2015
*Cortisol augmentation of a psychological treatment for warfighters with posttraumatic stress disorder: Randomized trial showing improved treatment retention and outcome.*

- PMID: 25212409
- DOI: 10.1016/j.psyneuen.2014.08.004
- N = 24 veterans.
- Randomized double-blind pilot.
- 30 mg oral hydrocortisone or placebo before PE sessions 3–10.

### Result
Hydrocortisone augmentation was associated with greater PTSD symptom reduction, explained substantially by better treatment retention. Baseline glucocorticoid sensitivity appeared relevant among responders.

Primary: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25212409/

## Study 2 — Lehrner et al. 2021
*A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of hydrocortisone augmentation of Prolonged Exposure for PTSD in U.S. combat veterans.*

- PMID: 34298438
- DOI: 10.1016/j.brat.2021.103924
- N = 60 combat veterans with PTSD.
- 30 mg oral hydrocortisone or placebo 30 min before exposure sessions.

### Result
PE produced robust improvement in both groups, but intent-to-treat analysis found **no overall additional PTSD-symptom benefit** from hydrocortisone. Exploratory signals suggested moderation by mild TBI/postconcussive symptoms and baseline glucocorticoid sensitivity.

Primary: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34298438/

## Timing counterexample
A separate spider-phobia RCT gave 20 mg hydrocortisone **after** exposure and found no augmentation of exposure outcome or one-month renewal, with an adverse subjective-renewal signal in a small seven-month follow-up subsample.

- PMID: 30245330
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30245330/

## FearPrime interpretation
Glucocorticoids are strongly timing/state-dependent.

Possible effects include:
- suppression of retrieval of old fear during exposure,
- modulation of new learning/consolidation,
- stress-system normalization,
- or, under other timing conditions, impairment of extinction retrieval.

Therefore:
`cortisol effect ≠ generic plasticity enhancement`

## Debug
The larger PTSD RCT outweighs the small positive pilot for a universal augmentation claim.

## FearPrime weight
**Mixed human clinical evidence; biomarker/timing hypothesis remains open.**
