# Brunet et al. 2018 — Pre-reactivation propranolol in chronic PTSD

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN CLINICAL RCT  
**Evidence grade:** A/B  
**Track:** reconsolidation / noradrenergic modulation

## Citation
Brunet A, Saumier D, Liu A, Streiner DL, Tremblay J, Pitman RK. *Reduction of PTSD Symptoms With Pre-Reactivation Propranolol Therapy: A Randomized Controlled Trial.* American Journal of Psychiatry. 2018;175(5):427-433.

- PMID: 29325446
- DOI: 10.1176/appi.ajp.2017.17050481
- Primary: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29325446/
- Trial: NCT01127568

## Design
- N = 60 adults with long-standing PTSD.
- Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled.
- Six weekly trauma-memory reactivation sessions.
- Propranolol/placebo administered before reactivation.

## Intervention as studied
The published protocol used:
- 0.67 mg/kg short-acting propranolol,
- plus 1.0 mg/kg long-acting propranolol,
- timed so beta-blockade was present around trauma-memory reactivation.

These are study-protocol metadata, not a personal treatment recommendation.

## Main result
Both groups improved, but the propranolol + reactivation group showed significantly larger post-treatment reductions in CAPS and PCL-S than placebo + reactivation.

## FearPrime interpretation
This is direct clinical evidence that noradrenergic modulation around trauma-memory retrieval can alter later clinical expression.

However, the appropriate claim is **not** “the traumatic memory was erased.” The trial measured symptoms after repeated reactivation sessions, not direct deletion of the original episodic memory trace.

## Conflict / replication context
Later meta-analyses reached conflicting conclusions. One 2022 meta-analysis pooling healthy and clinical reconsolidation studies reported moderate pooled benefit, while a PTSD-focused 2022 meta-analysis found no significant overall PTSD-symptom benefit and emphasized heterogeneity and small studies.

## Debug
- Repeated treatment combines retrieval, therapist contact, expectancy and beta-blockade.
- Propranolol affects arousal and physiological expression in addition to any reconsolidation mechanism.
- Reconsolidation boundary conditions are difficult to verify directly in clinical memories.

## FearPrime weight
**Important clinical comparator; mechanism remains contested.**
