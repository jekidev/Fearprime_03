# Transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation (tVNS) and fear extinction

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE  
**Evidence grade:** B/C  
**Track:** vagus / autonomic modulation / memory consolidation

## Burger et al. 2016

**Title:** The effects of transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation on conditioned fear extinction in humans

- PMID: 27222436
- DOI: 10.1016/j.nlm.2016.05.007
- Randomized controlled trial
- N = 31 healthy participants
- tVNS vs sham during extinction
- Retention tested 24 h later

### Result
tVNS accelerated **explicit** extinction learning measured by US expectancy, but did not improve explicit extinction retention 24 h later. Physiological extinction effects could not be clearly established.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27222436/

## Burger et al. 2017

**Title:** Mixed evidence for the potential of non-invasive transcutaneous vagal nerve stimulation to improve the extinction and retention of fear

- PMID: 28719827
- DOI: 10.1016/j.brat.2017.07.005
- N = 42 healthy students
- 3-day conditioning/extinction/retention study

### Result
tVNS again facilitated declarative/US-expectancy extinction, but did not clearly strengthen next-day retention or psychophysiological extinction indices.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28719827/

---

## Burger et al. 2019

**Title:** The effect of transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation on fear generalization and subsequent fear extinction

- PMID: 30986531
- DOI: 10.1016/j.nlm.2019.04.006
- N = 58 healthy students
- final tVNS n = 29, sham n = 29

### Result
tVNS did not change fear generalization, but reduced CS+ threat expectancy during extinction. Startle extinction was not significantly enhanced.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30986531/

## FearPrime interpretation

Human tVNS findings show a **repeated declarative-learning signal**, but not yet robust physiological or delayed-retention enhancement.

Therefore:
- explicit expectancy: promising
- autonomic fear response: uncertain
- durable extinction retention: uncertain
- clinical PTSD augmentation: not established

This distinction is essential because feeling/cognitively knowing that a cue is safe is not always equivalent to autonomic fear extinction.

## FearPrime weight
**Promising non-invasive mechanistic candidate; replication/clinical translation needed.**
