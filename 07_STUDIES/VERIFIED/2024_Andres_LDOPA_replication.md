# Andres et al. 2024 — Preregistered L-DOPA replication

**Status:** VERIFIED PRIMARY HUMAN REPLICATION  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Role:** critical counterweight to positive L-DOPA findings

## Citation
Andres E, Chuan-Peng H, Gerlicher AMV, Meyer B, Tüscher O, Kalisch R. *Replication study on the role of dopamine-dependent prefrontal reactivations in human extinction memory retrieval.* Nature Communications. 2024;15:2699.

- PMID: 38538636
- PMCID: PMC10973457
- DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-46936-y
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38538636/

## Design
- Preregistered human replication in healthy male participants.
- Tested post-extinction L-DOPA versus placebo.
- Examined next-day extinction-memory retrieval, contextual renewal, and spontaneous vmPFC reactivation patterns.
- Sufficient SCR data were available for approximately 66 participants in key analyses.

## Intervention as studied
- 150 mg levodopa + 37.5 mg benserazide after extinction.
- Placebo comparator.

## Main result
The replication:
- confirmed that spontaneous post-extinction vmPFC reactivations predicted later extinction-memory retrieval, although timing differed from the discovery work;
- **did not replicate a significant overall L-DOPA enhancement of extinction retrieval**;
- did not replicate L-DOPA enhancement of the number of vmPFC reactivations;
- did not find a clear advantage for L-DOPA in the preregistered renewal test.

Exploratory, non-preregistered analyses suggested possible moderation by baseline salivary alpha-amylase / sympathetic activity, but these require confirmation.

## FearPrime interpretation
The correct state of the evidence is not “L-DOPA reliably strengthens extinction.”

A better model is:
`L-DOPA effect = extinction quality × baseline state × paradigm × consolidation biology`

The mechanistic role of vmPFC post-learning reactivation appears more reproducible than a universal pharmacological main effect.

## Debug
This paper substantially lowers confidence in any simple protocol claiming that post-extinction L-DOPA will reliably improve later safety-memory retrieval.

**Mandatory counterevidence.** Any L-DOPA dossier that omits this replication is incomplete.
