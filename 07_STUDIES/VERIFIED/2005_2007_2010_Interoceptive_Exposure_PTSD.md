# Interoceptive exposure in PTSD — preliminary direct evidence

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN CLINICAL PILOT EVIDENCE  
**Evidence grade:** C  
**Track:** interoception / bodily prediction / exposure

## Rationale
PTSD can include conditioned fear of internal bodily sensations: tachycardia, breathlessness, dizziness, tension, heat, dissociation-like sensations or autonomic arousal.

Interoceptive exposure (IE) deliberately evokes safe bodily sensations so catastrophic predictions about those sensations can be tested.

## Wald & Taylor 2005
*Interoceptive exposure therapy combined with trauma-related exposure therapy for post-traumatic stress disorder: a case report.*

- PMID: 15844686
- Single case.
- Four IE sessions integrated with trauma-related imaginal/in-vivo exposure.
- PTSD and anxiety-sensitivity scores decreased and gains were maintained at 1- and 3-month follow-up.

Primary: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15844686/

## Wald & Taylor 2007 pilot
*Efficacy of interoceptive exposure therapy combined with trauma-related exposure therapy for posttraumatic stress disorder: a pilot study.*

- PMID: 17270392
- N = 7.
- Five of seven participants improved; four of the five assessed at one-month follow-up no longer met PTSD diagnostic criteria.
- Authors explicitly called for randomized controlled trials.

Primary: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17270392/

## Wald et al. 2010 — PTSD + chronic pain
*Posttraumatic stress disorder and chronic pain arising from motor vehicle accidents: efficacy of interoceptive exposure plus trauma-related exposure therapy.*

- PMID: 19941177
- DOI: 10.1080/16506070903183570
- N = 5 women.
- Four IE sessions followed by eight trauma-related exposure sessions.
- IE produced larger reductions in anxiety sensitivity; trauma-related exposure produced larger PTSD/avoidance reductions.

Primary: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19941177/

## FearPrime interpretation
IE maps naturally onto the FearPrime adaptive model:

`internal sensation → predicted catastrophe/threat → controlled sensation exposure → prediction error → revised interoceptive model`

This may be particularly relevant when fear of the **body's own defensive response** perpetuates the loop.

## Debug
Direct PTSD evidence is extremely small and uncontrolled. The evidence base for IE is much stronger in panic disorder than PTSD. Do not present IE as a proven standalone PTSD treatment from these studies.

## FearPrime weight
**Conceptually strong; direct PTSD evidence preliminary.**
