# Endocannabinoid system — human CB1 genetics and FAAH inhibition

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Track:** endocannabinoids / anandamide / CB1 / FAAH

## Heitland et al. 2012 — CNR1 genetics

**Title:** Failure to extinguish fear and genetic variability in the human cannabinoid receptor 1

- PMID: 23010766
- PMCID: PMC3565211
- DOI: 10.1038/tp.2012.90
- N = 150 healthy medication-free humans
- Paradigm: virtual-reality fear conditioning and extinction
- Outcomes: fear-potentiated startle and subjective fear

### Main result
Participants homozygous for the A allele at CNR1 promoter SNP rs2180619 failed to show the same extinction of fear-potentiated startle observed in G-allele carriers. Fear acquisition itself did not differ by genotype.

### FearPrime interpretation
This was an early direct human translation of the animal literature linking CB1 signaling specifically to extinction rather than acquisition.

Genetic association does not prove that pharmacologically increasing CB1 signaling will recreate the phenotype.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23010766/

## Mayo et al. 2020 — pharmacological FAAH inhibition

**Title:** Elevated Anandamide, Enhanced Recall of Fear Extinction, and Attenuated Stress Responses Following Inhibition of Fatty Acid Amide Hydrolase: A Randomized, Controlled Experimental Medicine Trial

- PMID: 31590924
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled experimental-medicine trial
- Healthy adults
- PF-04457845: 4 mg orally once daily for 10 days
- FAAH inhibitor group n = 16
- Placebo n = 29
- Fear/stress battery on days 9–10

Study dosing is literature metadata, not a personal-use protocol.

### Main result
FAAH inhibition:
- produced roughly a 10-fold elevation in baseline anandamide;
- enhanced 24-h fear-extinction recall;
- attenuated autonomic stress reactivity;
- reduced stress-induced negative affect.

### Mechanistic importance
This is much stronger evidence than simply observing circulating endocannabinoids after exercise because FAAH was experimentally manipulated.

Working pathway:
`FAAH inhibition → ↑ anandamide → CB1-related signaling → improved extinction-memory recall / stress regulation`

### Important limitation
PF-04457845 is an investigational FAAH inhibitor. This experiment was in healthy adults rather than patients with PTSD.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31590924/

## FearPrime weight
**High mechanistic/translational priority.**

The endocannabinoid track now has both:
1. human genetic evidence implicating CB1 in extinction, and
2. randomized human pharmacological evidence that raising anandamide through FAAH inhibition can strengthen later extinction recall.
