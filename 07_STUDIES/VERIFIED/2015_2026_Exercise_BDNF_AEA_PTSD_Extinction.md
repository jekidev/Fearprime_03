# Exercise as FearPrime extinction augmentation

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN CLINICAL + EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE  
**Evidence grade:** A/B  
**Track:** exercise / BDNF / anandamide / consolidation

## Powers et al. 2015 — pilot PE augmentation

**Title:** Exercise Augmentation of Exposure Therapy for PTSD: Rationale and Pilot Efficacy Data

- PMID: 25706090
- N = 9 PTSD
- Prolonged Exposure alone vs PE + exercise
- Exercise: 30 min moderate-intensity treadmill before PE
- Pilot reported larger PTSD improvement and higher BDNF in PE+exercise.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25706090/

Small pilot only.

## Crombie et al. 2021 — extinction + biomarkers in women with PTSD

**Title:** Exercise-induced increases in Anandamide and BDNF during extinction consolidation contribute to reduced threat following reinstatement

- PMID: 34280820
- DOI: 10.1016/j.psyneuen.2021.105355
- N = 35 women with PTSD
- Three-day acquisition → extinction → recall paradigm
- Randomized after extinction to:
  - 30 min moderate-intensity aerobic exercise, or
  - 30 min very-light walking control.

### Main result
Exercise-induced increases in peripheral **anandamide (AEA)** and **BDNF** statistically mediated the relationship between exercise and lower threat expectancy after reinstatement.

2-AG and HVA did not show the same mediation pattern.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34280820/

### FearPrime significance
This links two major project pathways:
`exercise → AEA + BDNF → extinction consolidation / relapse resistance`

The biomarkers are peripheral and mediation is not proof of central causal mechanism, but this is unusually coherent human translational evidence.

## Bryant et al. 2019 — BDNF genotype moderation

**Title:** Acute exercise-induced enhancement of fear inhibition is moderated by BDNF Val66Met polymorphism

- PMID: 30967530
- DOI: 10.1038/s41398-019-0464-z
- N = 70 non-clinical participants
- Intense exercise vs no exercise after extinction
- Exercise group showed less fear 24 h later overall.
- BDNF Val66Met moderated the effect; Met carriers showed less favorable exercise-associated fear recovery.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30967530/

## Bryant et al. 2023 — clinical PTSD RCT

**Title:** Augmenting trauma-focused psychotherapy for post-traumatic stress disorder with brief aerobic exercise in Australia: a randomised clinical trial

- PMID: 36436532
- DOI: 10.1016/S2215-0366(22)00368-6
- N = 130 PTSD
- 65 exposure + 10 min aerobic exercise
- 65 exposure + 10 min passive stretching
- Nine weekly exposure sessions

### Main result
At six months, exposure + exercise produced greater CAPS reduction than exposure + stretching:
- mean between-group difference ≈ 12.1 points
- moderate effect size ≈ 0.6.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36436532/

## 2023 category-generalization experiment

**Title:** Aerobic exercise after extinction learning reduces return of fear and enhances memory of items encoded during extinction learning

- PMID: 37065640
- N = 40
- Exercise immediately after extinction reduced later threat expectancy and improved memory for extinction-phase stimuli.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37065640/

## 2026 context-generalization study

**Title:** The effects of pre-extinction stress vs. physical exercise on contextual retrieval and generalization

- PMID: 41719892
- Published 2026
- Stress and exercise both changed physiological state, but did not produce a simple universal retrieval benefit.
- Exploratory outcomes suggested context- and sex-dependent changes in renewal/generalization.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41719892/

## FearPrime conclusion

Exercise currently has one of the most compelling **risk/complexity-to-evidence ratios** in the framework because it has:
- laboratory extinction evidence,
- PTSD-specific experimental evidence,
- biomarker convergence with AEA and BDNF,
- and a randomized clinical exposure-augmentation trial with durable 6-month benefit.

It still should not be reduced to “exercise raises BDNF, therefore it works”; cardiovascular arousal, norepinephrine, endocannabinoids, dopamine, mood, sleep and expectancy may all contribute.

## FearPrime weight
**Core non-pharmacological augmentation candidate.**
