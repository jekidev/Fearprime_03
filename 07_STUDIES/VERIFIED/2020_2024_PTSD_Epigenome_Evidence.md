# PTSD epigenetics / DNA methylation — evidence audit

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN MOLECULAR EVIDENCE  
**Evidence grade:** B/C for PTSD biology; D/E for extinction augmentation  
**Track:** epigenetics / methylation / immune-metabolic regulation

## Smith et al. 2020

**Title:** Epigenome-wide meta-analysis of PTSD across 10 military and civilian cohorts identifies methylation changes in AHRR

- PMID: 33235198
- DOI: 10.1038/s41467-020-19615-x
- N = 1,896 PTSD cases and trauma-exposed controls across 10 cohorts

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33235198/
Journal:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-19615-x

### Main result
PTSD was associated with blood DNA-methylation differences, with AHRR emerging as a notable signal. Lower AHRR methylation was associated with lower kynurenine, connecting epigenetic variation with immune/metabolic biology.

## Snijders et al. 2020

**Title:** Longitudinal epigenome-wide association studies of three male military cohorts reveal multiple CpG sites associated with post-traumatic stress disorder

- PMID: 31931860
- Clinical Epigenetics 2020;12:11

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31931860/

### Main result
Longitudinal military cohorts showed PTSD-associated methylation patterns across several genomic positions/regions, including signals involving immune-related pathways.

## Larger 2024 meta-analytic work
A later meta-analysis across 23 military and civilian cohorts further expanded the set of PTSD-associated methylation loci.

This improves confidence that PTSD correlates with reproducible epigenetic patterns at the population level, while not proving that these marks cause symptoms or extinction deficits.

## FearPrime interpretation

Epigenetics matters to FearPrime at two distinct levels:

### 1. PTSD state / susceptibility markers
DNA methylation may encode or reflect:
- trauma exposure,
- immune state,
- stress-hormone signaling,
- smoking/environmental exposures,
- cell-composition shifts,
- resilience/vulnerability.

### 2. Extinction-memory regulation
The separate HDAC/histone-acetylation literature — including the sodium-butyrate track — directly implicates chromatin regulation in extinction-memory consolidation.

These must not be conflated.

PTSD-associated blood methylation is not proof that manipulating that CpG improves extinction.

HDAC inhibition enhancing extinction is not global reversal of the PTSD epigenome.

## Debug
- Most human PTSD epigenetic studies use peripheral blood rather than brain tissue.
- Cell-type composition and environmental covariates are major confounders.
- Association does not establish direction of causality.
- Epigenetic signatures can be dynamic and context-specific.

## FearPrime weight
**Strong systems-biology relevance; direct intervention translation remains limited.**
