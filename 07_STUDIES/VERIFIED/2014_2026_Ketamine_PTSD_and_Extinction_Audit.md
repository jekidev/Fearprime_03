# Ketamine — PTSD efficacy versus fear-extinction augmentation

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN PTSD EFFICACY + UNPROVEN EXTINCTION-AUGMENTATION TRACK  
**Evidence grade:** A/B for symptom reduction; C/E for extinction mechanism

## Feder et al. 2014 — single infusion

**Title:** Efficacy of intravenous ketamine for treatment of chronic posttraumatic stress disorder: a randomized clinical trial

- PMID: 24740528
- N = 41 chronic PTSD
- Randomized double-blind crossover
- Ketamine 0.5 mg/kg IV vs midazolam 0.045 mg/kg
- Significant rapid PTSD symptom reduction at 24 h relative to midazolam

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24740528/

---

## Feder et al. 2021 — repeated infusions

**Title:** A Randomized Controlled Trial of Repeated Ketamine Administration for Chronic Posttraumatic Stress Disorder

- PMID: 33397139
- N = 30
- Six infusions over two weeks
- Ketamine 0.5 mg/kg IV vs midazolam 0.045 mg/kg
- Greater CAPS-5 reduction with ketamine
- 67% responders vs 20% control in the published trial
- Median loss of response among ketamine responders: ~27.5 days

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33397139/

---

## 2023 systematic review/meta-analysis

**Title:** The Impact of Ketamine for Treatment of Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder: A Systematic Review With Meta-Analyses

- PMID: 37776285
- Included six randomized trials
- Pooled results favored ketamine for CAPS/PCL symptom reduction

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37776285/

---

## 2025 treatment-resistant PTSD RCT

**Title:** Ketamine for treatment-resistant post-traumatic stress disorder: double-blind active-controlled randomised crossover study

- PMID: 41030011
- DOI: 10.1192/bjo.2025.10854
- N = 33
- Intramuscular racemic ketamine 0.5 or 1.0 mg/kg vs fentanyl 50 µg
- Ketamine, particularly 1 mg/kg, produced short-term PTSD symptom reduction; dissociative/cardiovascular effects were also observed.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41030011/

---

## Ketamine + exposure psychotherapy

### Written Exposure Therapy open-label study — 2025
- PMID: 40215385
- N = 14 started; 13 completed
- Six ketamine infusions plus five WET sessions
- Large within-group improvement at 12 weeks
- **No randomized psychotherapy-only comparator**, therefore it cannot establish ketamine augmentation.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40215385/

### Ketamine-enhanced Prolonged Exposure
- PMID: 38729297
- Published record is an RCT **protocol**, not outcome evidence.
- Design compares ketamine vs active placebo adjunctive to PE in veterans.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38729297/

## FearPrime interpretation

Ketamine has legitimate human evidence as a **rapid PTSD symptom treatment**.

But:
`ketamine reduces PTSD symptoms ≠ ketamine has been proven to enhance fear extinction`

The extinction rationale remains based mainly on preclinical plasticity/extinction findings plus ongoing/early combination trials.

## Current FearPrime status
- PTSD symptom efficacy: **supported**
- Exposure augmentation: **not established**
- Extinction-consolidation mechanism in humans with PTSD: **unproven**

## Debug
Symptom improvement could arise from antidepressant, network-plasticity, dissociative, salience, learning or other mechanisms without requiring improved extinction memory.

## FearPrime weight
**Important candidate, mechanism unresolved.**
