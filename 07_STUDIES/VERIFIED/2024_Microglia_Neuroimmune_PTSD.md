# Microglia / neuroimmune function in PTSD

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN PTSD BIOLOGY  
**Evidence grade:** B/C for PTSD biology; E for extinction augmentation  
**Track:** inflammation / neuroimmune regulation / microglia

## Citation
Bonomi R, Hillmer AT, Woodcock E, et al. *Microglia-mediated neuroimmune suppression in PTSD is associated with anhedonia.* Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences. 2024;121(35):e2406005121.

- PMID: 39172786
- PMCID: PMC11363315
- DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2406005121
- PTSD n = 15
- age-matched controls n = 15

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39172786/

## Design
Participants underwent TSPO PET imaging before and ~3 h after lipopolysaccharide (LPS), providing a dynamic challenge of central neuroimmune responsiveness rather than a static inflammatory snapshot.

## Main result
Compared with controls, the PTSD group showed a significantly **smaller LPS-induced increase in TSPO availability** across a prefrontal-limbic circuit.

The difference included amygdala, hippocampus and vmPFC signals. Greater suppression of this neuroimmune response correlated with worse anhedonic symptoms.

## FearPrime interpretation

This study challenges the simplistic model:

PTSD = globally excessive brain inflammation

At least in this experimental immune-challenge context, PTSD showed **suppressed dynamic microglial/TSPO responsivity** rather than simple overactivation.

Therefore FearPrime should distinguish:
- peripheral inflammatory markers,
- basal central immune state,
- dynamic microglial responsivity,
- acute immune challenge,
- symptom domains,
- and extinction-specific mechanisms.

## Direct extinction evidence
This experiment did **not** test fear extinction or exposure augmentation.

There is currently no basis in this ledger for claiming that generic anti-inflammatory or microglia-suppressing interventions will improve extinction.

## Debug
- Small sample.
- TSPO is an imperfect and non-specific proxy for microglial state.
- LPS challenge biology cannot be directly equated with chronic PTSD physiology.
- Anhedonia association does not establish causality.

## FearPrime weight
**Important corrective biology; not an extinction intervention.**
