# Acute inflammation and human fear extinction

**Status:** VERIFIED HUMAN RANDOMIZED EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE  
**Evidence grade:** B  
**Track:** inflammation / cytokines / interoception

## Pawlik et al. 2023

**Title:** Inflammation shapes neural processing of interoceptive fear predictors during extinction learning in healthy humans

- PMID: 36535608
- DOI: 10.1016/j.bbi.2022.12.010
- N = 95 healthy volunteers across two complementary studies.
- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled fMRI design.
- Experimental endotoxemia:
  - intravenous LPS 0.4 ng/kg,
  - or placebo.
- LPS administered before either acquisition or extinction depending on study arm.
- Two-day fear paradigm with interoceptive and exteroceptive aversive outcomes.

Study exposure is literature metadata, not a personal protocol.

## Main result
LPS produced robust inflammatory and emotional responses but did **not** directly alter conditioned fear formation or extinction on CS valence ratings.

However, when inflammation occurred before acquisition:
- extinction later showed enhanced neural responses specifically to predictors of interoceptive threat in core fear circuitry;
- unexpected re-exposure to the interoceptive aversive stimulus after extinction was rated more negatively.

Primary:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36535608/

## FearPrime interpretation

Inflammation can modify the **neural representation and salience of interoceptive fear** without necessarily producing a simple behavioral extinction deficit on every endpoint.

This is highly relevant to FearPrime's interoceptive model:

`inflammation/body state → stronger internal-threat representation → later extinction network burden`

but the study does not support a simple:
`inflammation = extinction failure`
rule.

## Translational caution
- Healthy sample, experimental acute inflammation.
- PTSD often involves chronic or recurrent low-grade inflammatory differences, which are biologically different from transient endotoxemia.
- Peripheral cytokine elevation does not map one-to-one onto microglial activation.

## FearPrime weight
**Strong mechanistic human evidence, especially for interoceptive threat.**
