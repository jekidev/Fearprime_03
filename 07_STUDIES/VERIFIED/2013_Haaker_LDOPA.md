# Haaker et al. 2013 — L-DOPA and context-independent extinction memory

**Status:** VERIFIED PRIMARY HUMAN + ANIMAL STUDY  
**Evidence grade:** B / translational  
**Population:** healthy men + mouse experiments

## Citation
Haaker J, Gaburro S, Sah A, et al. *Single dose of L-dopa makes extinction memories context-independent and prevents the return of fear.* PNAS. 2013;110(26):E2428-E2436.

- PMID: 23754384
- PMCID: PMC3696794
- DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1303061110
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23754384/

## Human design
- 40 right-handed healthy male volunteers randomized; one L-DOPA participant excluded, leaving N = 39.
- Conditioning and extinction occurred on day 1 in different contexts.
- Drug/placebo was given immediately after learning.
- Return-of-fear/context test occurred on day 2.
- Main physiological outcome: skin conductance response; fMRI was also used.

## Intervention as studied
- 150 mg levodopa + 37.5 mg benserazide, orally, immediately after extinction.
- Placebo comparator.

Study dose is literature metadata, not a personal dosing instruction.

## Main human result
Post-extinction L-DOPA reduced contextual renewal/return of conditioned fear. The effect was associated with:
- reduced amygdala response,
- increased vmPFC activity,
- enhanced post-extinction functional coupling between dopaminergic midbrain and vmPFC.

## Translational result
Mouse experiments also supported a dopamine-dependent effect on extinction-memory consolidation and return of fear.

## FearPrime interpretation
This study is the foundational evidence for treating dopamine as a **post-learning consolidation modifier**, not simply an acute anxiolytic.

The especially interesting claim is reduced context-dependency of extinction memory, because context-bound safety learning is a major mechanism of renewal.

## Debug / limitations
- Small human sample.
- Healthy males only.
- Laboratory conditioned fear is not clinical PTSD.
- Subsequent work has not consistently reproduced a simple main effect of L-DOPA, so this study must not be interpreted in isolation.

## FearPrime weight
**Important but not standalone proof.** Interpret together with Gerlicher 2019 and Andres 2024.
